SEE browser performance harness

Measures browser-side QR frame generation and image-based QR reconstruction. This is separate from the Node.js core benchmark. Run it on each browser/device you want to report.

The test creates real SVG QR frames, loads each frame as an image, decodes the images through SEE's QR adapter, and verifies the reconstructed envelope. Large transfers can take time. Keep the tab in the foreground and avoid other heavy workloads.
